# Steady

Steady is a calm, visual habit tracker designed to help you build consistency one day at a time. Each habit has a contribution-style history grid, making it easy to see your progress, recognize patterns, and keep a streak going without unnecessary clutter.

## Getting started

1. Open Steady and select **Create account**.
2. Enter your email address and choose a password.
3. Confirm your email if prompted, then sign in.
4. Select **New habit** and give your habit a name, schedule, and color.
5. Open the **Today** page whenever you complete a habit and select **Mark done**.

Your account keeps its own habits and history. You can sign in from another supported browser or device to access the same synchronized data.

## Today

The Today page shows the habits scheduled for the current day. Each square displays the habit name, frequency, color, and completion status.

- Select **Mark done** when you complete a habit.
- Select **Completed** again if you need to undo the check-in.
- The heading shows how many of today's scheduled habits are finished.

## History

The History page presents each habit as a Git-style activity grid. Filled squares represent completed days, while empty squares show days without a check-in.

You can switch between the last 26 weeks and the full 52-week history. The summary cards show your recent completion rate, current streak, best streak, and total habits finished today.

Select any available day in a grid to add or remove a past check-in.

## Managing habits

Use the menu on a habit card to:

- View details and notes
- Edit its name, schedule, or color
- Export its grid as a PNG image
- Change its position in the list
- Archive a habit without losing its history
- Permanently delete it

Archived habits can be restored from the **Archived habits** option in the footer.

## Notes

Open a habit's **Details & notes** page to record a short note for a particular date. Notes can capture what helped, what made the habit difficult, or anything you want to remember when reviewing your progress.

## Backups and exports

The footer provides several ways to keep or share your progress:

- **Export backup** downloads your Steady data as a JSON file.
- **Import backup** restores data from a previous Steady JSON export.
- **Export all grids as PNG** creates images of all active habit grids.
- The menu on an individual habit can export only that habit's grid.

Keep occasional JSON exports if you want a personal offline backup. Importing a backup replaces the data in the currently signed-in account, and Steady automatically downloads a copy of the previous state before completing the import.

## Themes and devices

Use the theme button near **New habit** to switch between light and dark mode. Steady is designed to work across desktop and mobile screen sizes.

## Privacy

Every account has its own private habit data. Signing into another account will display only that account's habits, history, and notes. Always sign out when using a shared device.
